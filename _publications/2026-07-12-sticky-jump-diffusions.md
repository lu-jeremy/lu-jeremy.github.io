excerpt: "Sticky Jump Diffusions (SJDs) are continuous-time Markov processes on R^d whose discrete anchors are token embeddings. In the forward process, anchors release mass at a hazard rate that diffuses in continuous space; time reversal pairs a score-driven SDE with a sticky jump kernel governed by flux balance. Scores and reverse hazards are estimated through Denoising Hazard Matching using simulation-free cross-entropy training. The framework unifies masked, continuous, and hybrid diffusion as limiting cases and explains features each treats as given. Beyond these limits, the unsticking kernel becomes a design space: cross-position blending corrupts each position toward a blend of its neighbors' clean values or embeddings, improving results on CIFAR-10, Text8, and Sudoku."
